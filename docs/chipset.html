Chipset

In EmulatR the chipset is the adapter between the CPU(s) and the devices whose accesses it routes. Every CPU load, store, and fetch enters the chipset through its system-bus seam; the chipset decodes the physical address and steers it to DRAM, to a chipset control/status register (CSR), to a PCI device, or to the non-existent-memory path that raises a machine check.

 

Two distinct pieces of silicon are involved on the Alpha 21264 (EV6/EV68) machines EmulatR targets, and it is important not to confuse them.

 

Core-logic chipset (north side)

The core-logic chipset is the memory controller plus PCI/AGP host bridge -- the Cchip (command/address, interrupts, arbitration), Dchip (data slice), and Pchip (PCI host bridge). Two generations appear across the supported platforms:

Taxonomy note (corrected 2026-06-16): the part numbers are 21272 = Tsunami and 21274 = Titan. "Typhoon" is the higher-bandwidth (dual-Dchip) variant of the 21272, NOT a separate part. Earlier code mislabeled the 21274 as "Typhoon"; DS15/DS25/ES45 are Titan machines.

 

South bridge (legacy I/O side)

Hanging off PCI hose 0 is the south bridge, which provides the legacy ISA plane: the 8259 interrupt controllers, 8254 timers, MC146818 real-time clock, 16550 UARTs, keyboard controller, and an IDE controller. The south bridge is platform-specific and independent of the core-logic generation:

 

•Cypress CY82C693 -- DS10 / DS20 (PC264 lineage).

•ALi M1543C -- ES40 / ES45.

 

EmulatR selects the south bridge by machine model in TsunamiChipset::wireDevices(); the ISA devices behind it sit at fixed ports and are reused unchanged regardless of which bridge is wired.
