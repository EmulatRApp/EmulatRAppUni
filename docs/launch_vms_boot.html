launch_vms_boot

Cold-boots EmulatR (DS10/EV6) and boots OpenVMS Alpha V8.2 from dqa1. Deployed to <run-dir>/tools/ by CMake POST_BUILD; lives in tools/ but operates on the run-dir root.

 

Usage

./launch_vms_boot.sh

 

Preflight

Verifies Emulatr.exe and the firmware are present and that the platform manifest (ds10_platform.win) references the OpenVMS ISO (alpha082.iso) -- otherwise dqa1 is not wired to OpenVMS and the script aborts.

 

Notes

Launches with --no-autoload (cold boot) and tees the console to boot_vms_<timestamp>.log. The SRM console listens on TCP 127.0.0.1:10023. At the >>> prompt: e pmem:80130000040 (smir = 0), then b dqa1. The triage pass greps memory, halt, and frontier lines.
