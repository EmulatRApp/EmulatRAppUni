Cypress

Cypress Semiconductor (PCI vendor ID 0x1080) is the maker of the CY82C693 "HyperCore" combination PCI-to-ISA bridge used as the south bridge on the DS10 and DS20 (the PC264 platform lineage). In EmulatR, "Cypress" and "CY82C693" refer to this part and its device model.

 

The CY82C693 is the south bridge for the platforms EmulatR drives end to end today, so it is the most exercised of the two bridge models. It provides the PCI-to-ISA bridge (function 0), an IDE controller (function 1), and a USB host (function 2), and fronts the legacy ISA devices.

 

The ES40 and ES45 do NOT use the Cypress -- their south bridge is the ALi M1543C. Do not assume Cypress for an ES-class machine.

 

The full part detail -- PCI identity, the multi-function layout, and the EmulatR device classes (Cy82C693IsaBridge, Cy82C693Ide) -- is on the dedicated part page.
